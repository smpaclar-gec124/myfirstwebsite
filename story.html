My Childhood

My Beginning

A small beginning, a life full of stories.

I was born on January 2005, in Cagayan de Oro City. I grew up in a small but fun village, and was cared for by my simple and loving family. I was surrounded by people who always supported me. Our home was filled with laughter, faith, and the kind of love that made me feel safe even in the most uncertain times.

My Family

My greatest source of strength.

My family has always been my biggest source of strength. They taught me the value of hard work, kindness, and faith. No matter what I go through, they continue to be my cheerleaders, my safe place, and my greatest inspiration. My father has worked abroad since I was a kid, and now I am studying in a different town, away from home. They may not always be around, but their love has always been there.

A Childhood Memory

My 5th Birthday

As a child, I was playful, but I was also very shy when it came to interacting with other people. I was comfortable around the people I knew, but being surrounded by unfamiliar faces was a completely different story.

I remember my fifth birthday, when my lola decided to throw a party for me at her house. She invited all the children in our village so we could eat, play, and celebrate together.

But Baby Shary was too stunned to speak.

Instead of enjoying the party, I became overwhelmed by the number of unfamiliar people in the house and started crying. I was simply too shy and overwhelmed by everything happening around me.

Seeing that I wasn't comfortable, my mom, along with my titos and titas, brought me back to our house, which was just beside my lola's. They brought my birthday cake and some balloons with us, and instead of having a big celebration, it became a small one—just me and the people I was most comfortable with.

They sang me “Happy Birthday,” and that was when I finally started smiling and enjoying my day.

Looking back, it was such a simple moment, but it says a lot about the little girl I was. I was playful and happy, but I found comfort in familiar faces and quiet moments. I may have been too shy for a house full of people, but surrounded by the people I loved, I felt safe enough to be myself.

Growing Up

Finding people who became family.

As I grew older, I slowly became more comfortable stepping outside my little circle. School became more than just a place where I learned—it was where I met people who would become an important part of my life.

Along the way, I met friends who eventually became like family to me. We grew up together, shared countless memories, supported each other through different stages of life, and witnessed each other change along the way. Some of these friendships have lasted through the years, and I am grateful that I am still close to many of them today.

Looking back, I think growing up was not only about becoming more independent or discovering who I was. It was also about finding people who made the journey more meaningful. The little girl who once cried because she was surrounded by unfamiliar faces eventually found people she could laugh with, be herself around, and call family.

What My Childhood Taught Me

“Looking back, I realize that the person I am today did not simply appear overnight.”

Many of my values, habits, and dreams began taking shape during these early years. My childhood may have been simple, but it was full of love, lessons, and moments that continue to guide me until now.

My School Years

The people, experiences, and choices that helped me discover who I am and what I want to become.

01

Elementary

Bugo Central School

My elementary years were filled with curiosity, play, and simple joys. It was where I learned how to read, write, and find excitement in the little things. I made my first friends, discovered my love for stories, and took my first steps toward the dreams I had—even if I didn’t know it yet.

02

Junior High School

Gusa Regional Science High School – Region X

JHS taught me discipline, independence, and the value of hard work. I stepped out of my comfort zone and started to build the confidence I needed for what was ahead.

03

Senior High School

Liceo de Cagayan University

Senior High School was a turning point for me. It was where I started to understand more about myself—my interests, strengths, and what I wanted for my future.

04

Becoming a Nursing Student

Mindanao State University – Iligan Institute of Technology

At first, I planned to take a different course and enrolled as a BS Psychology student in Liceo de Cagayan University. After thinking about my goals and what I really wanted for my future, I had a last-minute change of heart, withdrew my enrollment, and went to Iligan City to pursue BS Nursing in MSU–IIT.

I chose nursing because I wanted to be part of something bigger—to help, to care, and to make a difference in people’s lives. Nursing school is a challenging path to take, but also one of the most rewarding experiences of my life. I have learned how to be more disciplined, compassionate, patient, and resilient.

Each school witnessed and built a different part of me. Every experience—the good, the hard, and the unexpected—helped me get here. They shaped my choices, strengthened my heart, and brought me closer to the person I want to become.

04

The Story Ahead

I dream of exploring new places, experiencing different cultures, building a life with financial stability, and one day, following my dad to Norway or somewhere in Europe as a nurse. I want to be a nurse who leads with compassion, listens without judgement, and gives the best care I can. I hope to be someone other people can trust, someone who advocates for what is right, and someone who treats every person with dignity and respect.

Hopefully in the future, I will be able to achieve all of my dreams, help my family, explore the world, take better care of myself, and be filled with love that radiates to the people around me.
